# Phase 1 assumptions and limitations

- All concrete dimensions are entered in metres.
- Slab, beam, and column geometries are rectangular prisms.
- The result is a geometric volume in cubic metres.
- The optional allowance is user-selected and is applied as a simple percentage.
- No automatic dry-volume factor, mix ratio, cement quantity, aggregate quantity, or water quantity is assumed.
- No structural design or code-compliance conclusion is produced.
- The toolkit is educational and intended for preliminary quantity calculations. Verify project-specific requirements with qualified professionals and applicable standards before real-world use.
